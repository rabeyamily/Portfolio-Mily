About me

I'm Rabeya, a senior at NYU Abu Dhabi pursuing a degree in Computer Science, with minors in Economics and Interactive Media. I love building things that live at the boundary of technology and human experience — whether that's a data-driven web app, a generative art piece, or an economic model that tells a story.

My academic interests span machine learning, behavioral economics, and creative coding. Outside the classroom I'm a photographer and videographer, chasing light across Abu Dhabi's coastlines and city streets with my camera. I believe the same curiosity that makes good code also makes a great photograph.

I'm actively looking for exciting projects that challenge me to think across disciplines.
