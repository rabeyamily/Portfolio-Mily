About me

I'm Rabeya, a senior at NYUAD pursuing a degree in Computer Science, with minors in Economics and Interactive Media. I'm deeply interested in building ideas that sit at the intersection of technology, creativity, and human experience — whether that means designing a data-driven web application, or experimenting with generative art, reveals meaningful patterns and tells a compelling story.

My academic interests span machine learning, behavioral economics, human-centered design, and creative coding. I'm especially drawn to projects that combine analytical thinking with imagination, where technical systems can be used to better understand people, solve real problems, or create something beautiful and unexpected. I enjoy exploring how technology can be both functional and expressive.

Outside the classroom, I'm a photographer and videographer, often chasing light across Abu Dhabi's coastlines, desert landscapes, and city streets with my camera. Visual storytelling has taught me to notice detail, emotion, and perspective — skills that influence how I approach coding and design.
